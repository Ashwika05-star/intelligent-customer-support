# User Manual: ApexClean X9 Smart Robot Vacuum & Mop (Model: AC-X9)

## 1. Technical Specifications
- **Brand:** ApexClean
- **Model:** AC-X9
- **Suction Power:** 5,500 Pa (4 suction power levels: Quiet, Standard, Strong, Max)
- **Dustbin Capacity:** 400 mL dustbin + 3.0L Auto-Empty base bag (holds up to 60 days of dust)
- **Water Tank:** 250 mL electronically controlled water tank (3 water flow levels)
- **Navigation:** LiDAR LDS 360-degree laser mapping + 3D ToF obstacle avoidance
- **Battery & Runtime:** 5,200 mAh Lithium-ion battery (up to 180 minutes runtime on Quiet mode; covers up to 2,200 sq. ft.)
- **Threshold Climbing:** Can climb obstacles/thresholds up to 20mm (0.78 inches)
- **App Connectivity:** 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only (5 GHz Wi-Fi is NOT supported)
- **Voice Control:** Works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home

## 2. Initial Setup & Wi-Fi Pairing
1. Place the Auto-Empty dock against a flat wall with 1.5m clearance in front and 0.5m on both sides.
2. Download the `ApexClean Home` app (iOS & Android).
3. Ensure your phone is connected to a **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network**.
4. Press and hold both the **Power** and **Dock** buttons on the robot for 3 seconds until you hear *"Entering Wi-Fi network configuration"*.
5. Follow the on-screen pairing prompts in the app.

## 3. Maintenance & Filter Cleaning
- **HEPA Filter:** Wash with lukewarm water bi-weekly. **Must air-dry completely for 24 hours** before reinserting. Never operate with a wet filter.
- **Main Brush:** Remove hair and tangles weekly using the included cleaning comb.
- **Side Brush:** Replace every 3 to 6 months depending on wear.
- **Mop Pads:** Machine washable (up to 50 washes). Replace every 3 months.

## 4. Common Error Codes & Troubleshooting
- **Error 1 (Bumper Stuck):** Gently tap the front bumper to dislodge any stuck debris.
- **Error 4 (Main Brush Jammed):** Flip the robot, unclip the brush guard, and remove entangled wires or hair.
- **Error 8 (LiDAR Turret Blocked):** Inspect the top rotating laser dome for obstructions; clean lens with a dry microfiber cloth.
- **Robot keeps losing map:** Ensure the dock has not been moved. Turn off mirrored baseboards or cover floor-to-ceiling glass doors below 15cm.
- **Hard Reset:** Open the top cover, press and hold the small Reset pinhole button with a paperclip for 5 seconds.
