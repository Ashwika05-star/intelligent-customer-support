# User Manual: ErgoComfort Max Ergonomic Office Chair (Model: EC-MAX)

## 1. Product Specifications & Dimensions
- **Brand:** ErgoComfort
- **Model:** EC-MAX
- **Weight Capacity:** Up to 330 lbs (150 kg)
- **Recommended User Height:** 5'2" to 6'4" (157 cm to 193 cm)
- **Materials:** Breathable Korean mesh backrest, high-density molded memory foam seat cushion, heavy-duty aluminum alloy base, Class 4 gas lift cylinder.
- **Dimensions:**
  - Seat Width: 20.5 inches (52 cm)
  - Seat Depth: 19.3 inches (49 cm)
  - Seat Height Range: 18.5" to 22.4" (47 cm to 57 cm)
  - Recline Angle: 90° to 135° with 3 lockable tilt positions (90°, 115°, 135°)

## 2. Ergonomic Adjustments
- **4D Armrests:** Adjust height (up/down by 3 inches), depth (forward/backward), width (inward/outward), and pivot angle (20° left/right).
- **Dynamic Lumbar Support:** 2-way adjustable lumbar module moves up/down by 2 inches and depth-adjusts automatically based on user posture.
- **Seat Depth Adjustment (Seat Slider):** Pull the lever on the left side of the seat to slide the seat cushion forward or backward.
- **Headrest Adjustment:** Height adjusts by 4 inches; tilt pivots by 45°.

## 3. Assembly Instructions & Hardware
- **Assembly Time:** Approximately 15 to 20 minutes (1 person).
- **Tools Included:** 1x M5 Allen wrench, 10x M6 screws, 4x M8 washers.
- **Step 1:** Insert the 5 caster wheels firmly into the aluminum base until they click.
- **Step 2:** Place the Class 4 gas lift cylinder into the center of the base.
- **Step 3:** Fasten the mechanism to the bottom of the seat cushion using 4x M6 bolts.
- **Step 4:** Mount the backrest to the mechanism using 3x M8 bolts.
- **Step 5:** Lower the assembled seat onto the gas lift cylinder and apply firm downward pressure.

## 4. Care & Troubleshooting
- **Squeaking Noise:** Apply standard silicone spray lubricant (do NOT use WD-40) to the tilt mechanism pivot points under the seat.
- **Gas Lift Not Holding Height:** If the chair slowly sinks, the gas lift seal has failed. Contact NovaMart support with order ID for a free replacement gas cylinder under the 5-year mechanical warranty.
- **Cleaning:** Vacuum mesh with a soft brush attachment. Spot clean foam cushion with mild soapy water and a damp cloth. Do not use harsh bleach.
