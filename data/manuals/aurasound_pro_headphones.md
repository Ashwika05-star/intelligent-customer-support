# User Manual: AuraSound Pro ANC Wireless Headphones (Model: AS-900)

## 1. Specifications Overview
- **Brand:** AuraSound
- **Model:** AS-900 (AuraSound Pro)
- **Bluetooth Version:** 5.3 (Supports Multipoint pairing up to 2 devices simultaneously)
- **Active Noise Cancellation (ANC):** Hybrid ANC up to -38dB with 3 modes (High ANC, Transparency Mode, Off)
- **Battery Life:**
  - Up to **45 hours** with ANC OFF (at 60% volume)
  - Up to **32 hours** with ANC ON
- **Charging Time:** 1.5 hours via USB-C (Quick charge: 10 minutes gives 5 hours of playback)
- **Water Resistance:** IPX4 splash-proof (resistant to sweat and light rain; NOT submersible)
- **Audio Codecs:** LDAC, AAC, SBC
- **Weight:** 245 grams
- **In The Box:** Headphones, USB-C Charging Cable (1m), 3.5mm Aux Audio Cable, Hard Travel Case, Quick Start Guide.

## 2. Pairing & Multi-Point Connection
1. Power on by holding the Power button for 3 seconds. The LED flashes blue/red.
2. Open Bluetooth settings on your device and select `AuraSound Pro AS-900`.
3. **To pair a second device:** Pause playback on Device 1, hold the Bluetooth button for 3 seconds until LED flashes, select on Device 2. Both devices are now linked.

## 3. Button Controls
- **Power Button:**
  - Hold 3s: Power On / Off
  - Tap once: Play / Pause / Answer Call
  - Double tap: Next track
  - Triple tap: Previous track
- **ANC Button:** Cycle through High ANC -> Ambient Transparency -> Normal (Off).
- **Volume Rocker:** Tap `+` or `-` to adjust volume; hold 2s for voice assistant (Siri / Google Assistant).

## 4. Troubleshooting & Factory Reset
- **Headphones will not turn on:** Plug in via original USB-C cable for at least 15 minutes. Check if red charging indicator illuminates.
- **Audio stuttering or disconnection:** Ensure distance is within 10 meters (33 ft) without thick concrete walls. Disconnect other Bluetooth devices.
- **Factory Reset Procedure:**
  1. Power off the headphones.
  2. Hold both the **Power Button** and **Volume Down (-)** buttons simultaneously for **7 seconds**.
  3. The LED will flash pink three times and turn off. Reset is complete.
