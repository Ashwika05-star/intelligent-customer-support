# NovaMart Official Return & Refund Policy

## 1. General Return Window
- Customers may return most new, unopened, and undamaged items within **30 days of confirmed delivery** for a full refund.
- Items must be returned in their original packaging, including all accessories, manuals, and documentation.
- A return shipping fee of **$5.99** applies unless the return is due to a NovaMart error, defective product upon arrival, or incorrect shipment.

## 2. Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items
The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances:
- Digital downloads, software licenses, and e-gift cards.
- Consumables and hygiene-sensitive products (e.g., ear tips, opened personal care items).
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at checkout.
- Customized or personalized items.

## 3. Refund Processing & Timelines
- Once an item arrives at the return fulfillment center, inspect takes **2 to 3 business days**.
- After inspection approval:
  - **Credit / Debit Cards:** Refund appears in **5 to 7 business days** depending on the issuing bank.
  - **Store Credit / Gift Card:** Available immediately upon approval.
  - **PayPal / UPI:** Refund processed within **24 to 48 hours**.
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item arrived defective or broken.

## 4. Damaged or Defective Items (Dead on Arrival)
- If an item arrives broken, damaged in transit, or defective, customers must report it within **48 hours of delivery**.
- NovaMart will provide a prepaid return shipping label and issue an immediate replacement or full refund upon scan by the carrier.

## 5. Escalation & Special Exceptions
- Returns beyond the 30-day window (up to 45 days) require manager authorization and may incur a **15% restocking fee**.
- Inquiries regarding missing refunds exceeding 10 business days must be escalated directly to human support with the transaction ARN (Acquirer Reference Number).
