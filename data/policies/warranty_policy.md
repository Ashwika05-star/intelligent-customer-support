# NovaMart Official Warranty & Guarantee Policy

## 1. Standard Limited Warranty Coverage
- All electronics and smart appliances sold on NovaMart come with a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** from the date of purchase.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware malfunctions, internal component failures, and electrical faults under normal intended use.

## 2. Exclusions & What Is NOT Covered
The warranty does **not** cover:
- Accidental physical damage (e.g., cracked screens, broken casings, drops).
- Liquid submersion or water damage (unless the product explicitly states an IPX8 immersion rating).
- Unauthorized repairs, modifications, jailbreaking, or third-party tampering.
- Normal cosmetic wear and tear (scratches, fabric discoloration, scuffs).
- Damage resulting from using non-certified third-party chargers or improper voltage.

## 3. Extended Protection Plans (NovaCare+)
- Customers can purchase **NovaCare+ 2-Year or 3-Year Protection** within 30 days of product purchase.
- NovaCare+ includes accidental drop protection, liquid damage repair, and battery replacement if maximum capacity drops below 70%.

## 4. How to File a Warranty Claim
1. Provide the original **Order ID** and product **Serial Number**.
2. Submit a brief description and photos/video demonstrating the defect.
3. If approved, NovaMart issues a prepaid return shipping label.
4. Repair turnaround time is **7 to 10 business days**. If the product cannot be repaired, a brand-new replacement unit or store credit equal to the original purchase value will be issued.
