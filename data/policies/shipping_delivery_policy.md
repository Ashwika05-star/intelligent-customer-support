# NovaMart Official Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods & Estimated Delivery Times
- **Standard Shipping (Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99):** Delivered within **3 to 5 business days**.
- **Express Shipping ($9.99 flat rate):** Delivered within **2 business days** (orders must be placed before 2:00 PM EST).
- **Next-Day Priority ($19.99):** Guaranteed delivery on the next business day (orders placed Monday through Thursday before 1:00 PM EST).
- **International Shipping ($24.99+):** 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance.

## 2. Order Processing & Tracking
- Orders are processed and dispatched within **24 hours** on business days (Monday to Friday, excluding national holidays).
- Once shipped, a tracking number and courier link (FedEx, UPS, or USPS) are automatically emailed and reflected in the user account.
- Status changes: `Processing` -> `Dispatched` -> `In Transit` -> `Out for Delivery` -> `Delivered`.

## 3. Delayed or Lost Shipments
- If a package has not moved for more than **5 consecutive days**, NovaMart initiates a formal courier trace.
- If a shipment is confirmed lost or remains undelivered after **10 business days past the expected delivery date**, customers may choose between an immediate free replacement or a 100% full refund.

## 4. Address Changes & Redirects
- Address modifications can only be made within **60 minutes** of placing the order.
- Once the package status is `In Transit`, customers must contact the carrier directly or request support escalation to attempt a carrier intercept ($10 reroute fee may apply).

## 5. Damaged Packages
- If the outer packaging arrives severely damaged, customers are advised to take photos before opening and notify support within **48 hours** for immediate resolution.
